Durable synchronization / jobs

Synchronization runs

Create tracked runs, commit batches page-by-page, and resume from the next uncompleted page after an interruption.

A run is pending until its first page, running while more pages remain, failed if an operation fails, and completed after the final committed page. A page update and its checkpoint share one database transaction.
